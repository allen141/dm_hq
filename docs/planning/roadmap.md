# Roadmap

The roadmap is outcome-based and intentionally avoids dates until capacity and delivery expectations are known.

## Phase 0 — Foundation (current)

- Establish documentation, CI, and decision-record practices.
- Interview or observe DMs preparing and running sessions.
- Define primary personas and critical live-play workflows.
- Agree on product principles and first-release boundaries.
- Create low-fidelity prototypes for the three components.
- Decide the initial architecture and development stack.

**Exit signal:** the team can describe a narrow end-to-end workflow, demonstrate it in a prototype, and explain the major technical choices.

## Phase 1 — Archive foundation

Create campaigns; capture, link, tag, and search notes and entities; establish player visibility; and export campaign data.

**Candidate outcome:** a DM can keep useful campaign knowledge in DM HQ and reliably find it again.

## Phase 2 — Session workspace

Assemble Archive content into a session, run it from a focused Dashboard, capture notes, and promote outcomes back to the campaign.

**Candidate outcome:** a DM can prepare and run a non-combat session without switching note tools.

## Phase 3 — Encounter workflow

Prepare encounters from campaign characters, run initiative and combat state, and save results into session history.

**Candidate outcome:** a DM can move from a session scene into combat and back without recreating participants or outcomes.

## Phase 4 — Refinement

Potential investments include collaboration, importers, richer player views, offline resilience, ruleset extensions, and reusable templates. These are hypotheses, not commitments.
