# DM HQ

**Build the world. Find what matters. Keep the session moving.**

DM HQ is a workspace for Dungeon Masters who need their preparation and live-play tools to behave like one coherent system. It aims to reduce time spent hunting through notes, reconstructing context, and managing combat state.

## Three connected components

### The Archive

The long-term memory of a campaign: people, places, factions, lore, plot threads, session notes, and player resources. [Explore The Archive](product/archive.md).

### The Dashboard

The DM's single-pane-of-glass during play: the current scene, relevant context, prepared material, active characters, and fast access to deeper notes. [Explore The Dashboard](product/dashboard.md).

### The Battlefield

A purpose-built encounter workspace for combatants, initiative, health, conditions, and repeated actions that otherwise interrupt combat. [Explore The Battlefield](product/battlefield.md).

## Current status

DM HQ is in **discovery and documentation**. These documents are a starting point, not a final specification. The immediate goals are to:

1. understand the jobs a DM needs to complete before and during a session;
2. define the shared campaign concepts connecting all three components;
3. identify a narrow, testable first release; and
4. record architecture choices before implementation.

See the [roadmap](planning/roadmap.md) and [open questions](planning/open-questions.md) for the next decisions.

!!! note "Working vocabulary"

    “DM” is the person preparing and running the game. “Player” is a participant who may see deliberately published campaign material. Shared campaign vocabulary remains ruleset-neutral, while the first Archive ruleset experience targets 2014 D&D 5e under [ADR 0001](decisions/0001-2014-5e-first.md).
