# The Archive

The Archive is the campaign's durable knowledge base and preparation space. It serves both private DM work and intentionally published player resources.

## Core jobs

- Capture an unstructured idea without interrupting creative work.
- Organize people, places, factions, items, events, and plot threads.
- Link related information and follow relationships naturally.
- Plan scenes and story beats without forcing one storytelling method.
- Record session outcomes and preserve campaign history.
- Publish selected, spoiler-safe material to players.
- Find information through search, filters, links, and recent activity.

## Candidate first-release capabilities

- Campaign workspaces.
- Markdown notes with tags and links.
- Structured pages for NPCs and locations.
- Private, draft, and player-visible publication states.
- Full-text search.
- Session records linked to campaign entities.
- A simple player-readable campaign portal.
- Export and backup of campaign content.

## Key workflows

**Prepare:** create or link notes, associate NPCs and locations, and collect them into a planned session.

**Record:** capture what happened and promote important changes into durable facts or future threads.

**Publish:** select safe information, preview it as a player, and publish it without exposing adjacent private content.

## Open design concerns

- How much structure helps without making capture feel like data entry?
- Should links be wiki-style, typed relationships, or both?
- What version history and recovery guarantees are necessary?
- Is player access anonymous, invitation-based, or account-based?
- Which import and export formats are essential?
