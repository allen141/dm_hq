# ADR 0001: Use 2014 D&D 5e as the first ruleset

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-07-27
- **Owners:** Product

## Context

The Archive needs concrete templates and workflows to validate progressive structure, session preparation, and retrieval during play. A ruleset-neutral first experience would avoid an early commitment but would make those workflows less specific and harder to evaluate.

The project does not expect to support another ruleset until the product is mature. The choice of an initial ruleset therefore needs to be explicit while preserving shared campaign concepts that are not specific to game mechanics.

## Decision drivers

- Provide a coherent first experience for a known style of campaign.
- Make templates, session records, NPCs, and encounters concrete enough to test.
- Avoid expanding the first release into multiple rulesets.
- Preserve a boundary between campaign knowledge and ruleset-specific fields.
- Respect licensing and avoid becoming a character builder or rules compendium.

## Options considered

### Start ruleset-neutral

Build only generic campaign concepts and defer ruleset-specific templates. This reduces initial coupling but makes the first experience less useful and less representative of the intended product.

### Start with 2014 D&D 5e

Use the 2014 fifth-edition rules as the first template and integration target while keeping shared Archive concepts ruleset-neutral.

### Support 2014 and 2024 rules together

Model both rules generations from the beginning. This increases the initial content, validation, migration, and terminology scope before the core Archive workflow has been proven.

## Decision

DM HQ will use 2014 D&D 5e as its first ruleset implementation. Initial Archive templates and workflows may use 2014 5e terminology and fields.

Campaigns, entities, notes, relationships, claims, revisions, sessions, and publications remain shared product concepts. Ruleset-specific data must be identifiable as such rather than being assumed to apply to every Archive record.

Support for the 2024 rules or another game system requires a later product decision. It is not part of the initial roadmap.

## Consequences

- The first-release Archive workflow can be tested with recognizable 2014 5e records.
- 2014 5e templates receive priority over generic multi-ruleset configuration.
- The product must distinguish open, licensed, and user-provided content before importing or redistributing rules resources.
- DM HQ will not implement a full character builder or replace purchased rules content.
- Some future ruleset work may require migrations, but early concepts should not prevent that evolution.
- The decision may be revisited after the 2014 5e product and Archive model are mature.
